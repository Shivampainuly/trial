01 / CARDIOLOGY

Heart care with precision.

Specialist cardiology consultation supported by focused cardiac assessment and monitoring services.

ECG

An electrocardiogram records the heart's electrical activity and rhythm. It is a quick, non-invasive assessment commonly used to support evaluation of chest symptoms, palpitations and other cardiac concerns.

2D Echo

Two-dimensional echocardiography uses ultrasound to create moving images of the heart. It helps specialists assess cardiac structure, chamber function, valves and overall pumping performance.

TMT

A treadmill test monitors the heart while exercise intensity increases. Heart rate, rhythm, blood pressure and ECG changes are observed to support assessment of exercise-related cardiac symptoms.

Holter

Holter monitoring continuously records the heart's electrical activity during normal daily life, typically over an extended period. It can help capture intermittent rhythm changes that may not appear during a short ECG.

Ambulatory BP

Ambulatory blood-pressure monitoring measures blood pressure at regular intervals while you go about your usual day and night. The readings help specialists understand blood-pressure patterns beyond a single clinic measurement.
