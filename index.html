04.—FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

The practical details patients usually want to know before visiting Vista Clinics.

What are the clinic consultation hours?
Monday – Saturday: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM (last registration: 8:30 PM). Sunday: Closed.

ECG, Echo, TMT, FibroScan and lab draws are available during clinic hours. Endoscopy and colonoscopy are scheduled separately by appointment. Online booking is open 24/7 for next-day slots.
Google says “Closed”. Can I still book?
Yes. “Closed” refers to physical walk-in consultation hours. Our digital front desk is open 24/7. Use the Book Appointment button or request a callback for the next available evening slot.
How do I book an appointment?
The fastest way is through Book Appointment: choose Cardiology or Gastroenterology, select a date/time, enter your details and confirm. You can also call 090300 14422 during clinic hours. For booking, we need the patient’s name, age, gender, mobile, preferred doctor (or “Any Specialist”) and reason for visit.
Can I book for a family member?
Yes. Use “Booking for someone else?” on the form. Enter their name and age, and use your mobile for OTP/confirmation. Please bring their previous prescriptions and reports.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, subject to slot availability. Pre-booking is recommended because it helps minimise waiting, confirm specialist availability and prepare diagnostic slots such as Echo, TMT or FibroScan.
Can I get tests done the same evening?
Yes — this is one of our key conveniences. If the doctor advises testing during your 6–9 PM consultation, ECG, Echo, FibroScan, BP checks and blood draws can be done immediately when feasible. TMT, Holter and ABPM are scheduled according to preparation and slot availability. Reports are delivered digitally, typically within 2 hours for ECG/Echo/TMT and within 24 hours for Holter/ABPM/Histopathology.
What should I bring for my first visit?
Bring previous prescriptions or discharge summaries, recent blood and imaging reports, your current medication list and an insurance card/ID if applicable. A referral is only needed when your specific policy requires one.
Where is Vista Clinics and is it accessible?
Vista Clinics is on the 3rd Floor, Sri Nilayam, Shiva Sai Colony, Sevalal Nagar, Moosapet, Hyderabad 500018, opposite Moosapet Metro Station. There is lift access, a wheelchair at reception and accessible clinic facilities. Search “Vista Clinics Moosapet” on Google Maps or use Get Directions.
